Developed a color detection system alongside my triplet brothers that can reliably identify colors by dynamically compensating for ambient light. Inspired by spectrometers used by astronomers, we paired a cheap 10-data channel spectral sensor with an Arduino Nano board to acquire data.

The project built experience with Random Forest classification models, spectral sensors, NumPy, data processing techniques, I2C communication, scikit-learn libraries, and calibration methods. While the approach is not FTC-legal due to pre-computation constraints, it gave us experience in both hardware and software development.

The resume adds that the work used cosine similarity, Random Forest classification, scikit-learn, and SoftMax comparison with a tuned temperature constant. It also notes graphical analysis on data and probability algorithms to compare each color detection method.
