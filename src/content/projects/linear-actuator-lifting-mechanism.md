Robotics teams are limited to eight motors and an 18 inch sizing restriction in all three dimensions. With seven of our eight motors being used for drivetrain, intake, and scoring, I had to build a gearbox underneath our robot to power both linear actuators simultaneously.

This was challenging because the gearbox system was hard to fit, and the traditional gears had to be replaced with bevel gears to account for the one-motor system. The design was robust because it had one-directional back-drivability and was capable of supporting the robot's weight without powering the motors.

The resume adds that I refined positioning of the motor, shafts, and U-channels in Fusion 360 to make the robot compatible with the rest of the robot and FTC design constraints.
